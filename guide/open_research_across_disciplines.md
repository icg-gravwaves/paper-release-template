# Open research across disciplines

This template is written for papers in LaTeX, as in physics, astronomy and
mathematics. Other fields have their own established open research
practices, preprint servers and data repositories. This page notes some of
them, as pointers rather than guidance; it is not a complete list.

| Field | Preprint servers | Data repositories | Other practices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Physics, astronomy, mathematics, computer science | arXiv | Zenodo; field archives and data centres | Software papers (e.g. JOSS) |
| Engineering | arXiv, TechRxiv | Zenodo, Figshare | Artefact evaluation (e.g. ACM) |
| Life sciences and medicine | bioRxiv, medRxiv | Dryad, Figshare, GEO, ENA, EGA | Study registration; reporting guidelines (EQUATOR); protocols.io; RRIDs |
| Psychology and social sciences | PsyArXiv, SocArXiv, SSRN, OSF Preprints | OSF, UK Data Service, ICPSR | Preregistration; registered reports |
| Humanities | Humanities Commons; institutional repositories | Institutional and national archives | |

General-purpose repositories (Zenodo, OSF, Figshare) and institutional
repositories accept outputs from any field.

Many fields write papers in a word processor rather than LaTeX, and some
use tools that combine code and manuscript in one document, such as R
Markdown and Quarto. This template does not cover those workflows.
